Road Safety & SDGs

This digital controller improves road safety by providing predictable, synchronized lighting:

  • Indicators communicate driver intentions to turn.
  • Brake lights immediately warn vehicles behind.
  • Hazard lights provide synchronized warning signals (overriding individual indicators).
  • Automatic blinking ensures consistent timing using a digital clock.

Aligns with: SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-being - reducing accidents) and SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure).
